Choicer Voicer Export

Version 1.0.0

A companion tool for The Choicer Voicer that rebuilds the dubs you record in the game: your takes laid over the backing track and the original video, then exports them as a shareable video file.

Built by Joseph Slade.
The Choicer Voicer is made by YeahMaybe; voice packs belong to their respective authors. This tool only reads the files the game has already written to your own computer. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the game's developers.

Video encoding is handled by ffmpeg (LGPL/GPL).
