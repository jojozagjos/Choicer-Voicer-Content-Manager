Choicer Voicer Content Manager

Version 1.1.0

This app reads the dubs you have already recorded in The Choicer Voicer and turns them into video files. It never changes the game or your recordings.

  1. Record a dub in the game first. Play a voice pack and record your lines. The game saves every take automatically. Nothing shows up here until you have done this at least once.
  2. Open this app. It finds the game's data folder on its own. If it cannot, open Settings and point it at the folder containing packs_voice.
  3. Pick a pack from the list on the left. The first time you open one it spends a few seconds preparing the video for preview. That only happens once per pack.
  4. Choose a recording session. If you dubbed the same pack more than once, each attempt is listed with its date and how many lines it has.
  5. Press play to hear your dub over the original video, then adjust anything that sounds off.
  6. Hit Export video, choose your settings, and save.

Every line has its own controls in the list on the right:

  • Yours / Original / Off swaps a single line between your recording, the original dialogue, or silence. Handy when one line did not come out well.
  • Volume raises or lowers just that line. Type a number for an exact value.
  • Timing shifts a line earlier or later in milliseconds if your take starts slightly off. Negative numbers are earlier.
  • Music and Dub at the bottom set the overall balance between the backing track and your voice.

In the export window:

  • MP4 works everywhere. Use WebM only if you specifically need it.
  • Vertical 9:16 reformats for Shorts, TikTok and Reels, filling the sides with a blurred copy of the video instead of black bars.
  • Burn captions draws the script onto the video permanently. Leave it off and tick the .srt box instead if you would rather add captions in an editor.
  • Normalise dub loudness evens out lines recorded too quietly or too loudly.
  • Selected line only exports a single line as a short clip. Click a line first.

Exports run one at a time. Queue as many as you like and they will work through in order.

  • No packs listed. The game folder is wrong, or you have not recorded a dub yet. Check the folder in Settings.
  • A pack shows "no dubs yet". That pack is installed but you have not recorded for it. Do that in the game first.
  • Export button is greyed out. The pack is still loading. Wait for the spinner to finish, then try again.
  • Preview is black. Switch packs and back again to rebuild the preview. If it keeps happening, tell me on Discord.
  • Export failed. Usually a full disk or a folder you do not have permission to write to. Try exporting somewhere like your Videos folder.
  • Windows warned about the app on first run. That is because the app is not signed with a paid certificate. Click More info then Run anyway.
