Choicer Voicer Content Manager

Version 1.1.2

This app lets you create and export modpacks for The Choicer Voicer, duh.

Exporting a dub

  1. Open Export and pick a pack. It takes longer to open the first time.
  2. Choose a recording session if you dubbed the same pack more than once.
  3. Press play, adjust anything, then Export video.

Building a pack

  1. Click Content, then on the sidebar choose which type of pack. Click Create new.
  2. Fill it with content. Dub packs get the full timeline editor. Click The Editor tab to learn more.

Already have a pack someone sent you? Unzip it and drop the folder straight onto the Content tab. The app works out what kind it is and installs it.

Dub packs open in a timeline editor.

Working the timeline

  • Click a clip to select it. Clicking empty space moves the playhead there and clears the selection. Dragging along the ruler at the top scrubs the playback.
  • Click in place for a moment, then drag to cut a new clip in the video.
  • Drag the bar, along the top of a clip to move it.
  • Drag a clip's edge to adjust the start and stop times.
  • Right or middle drag to pan.
  • Scroll to zoom and shift and scroll to pan.
  • Space plays and pauses the video. ← → steps two seconds, shift + ← → steps a single frame.

The buttons

  • Caption here creates a new clip to caption at the playhead.
  • Captions toggles captions during playback in the editor only.
  • Trim video cuts the video down to a range. See below.
  • Backing track lets you create a backing track for the clips to go on top of. Highly recommended.
  • Fit zooms the timeline back out to the whole video.
  • The volume slider is for listening in the editor only. It changes nothing in the pack nor export.

Trimming the video

Clicking Trim Video opens a panel to trim the video. You can drag the two handles, type the times into From and To, or press Use playhead for either end.

  • Trimming the video so that clips fall on the outside of the selection automatically moves the clips to the closest edge.
  • Both Undo and Redo work for undoing a trim.

Backing Track

Once a pack has a backing track, a second lane appears under the timeline showing its waveform against the same stretch of time.

  • Listening to switches between the two. Video plays the original audio Backing track plays the backing track by itself. Only one is ever audible in the editor. These buttons do not affect the export
  • AI separate uses HTDemucs to split the whole soundtrack into vocals, drums, bass and other, then rebuilds the backing track without the vocal stem. The first use downloads about 95 MB. Audio stays on your computer. It requires 64-bit Windows and a Vulkan-capable graphics card.
  • Muffle muffles the clip. It needs no download and stays available for lower-end computers.
  • Silence removes it completely, which is cleaner but sounds like the audio dropped out outside of clips.

AI separation removes singing as well as dialogue.

Pictures

For each clip, an image appears alongside it in the dubbing gamemode.

  • Grab frame takes the video frame showing right now in the timeline.
  • Upload picks a picture file from your computer.
  • Reuse reuses a photo.

Lines are never given each other's pictures automatically, even with the same character. Reuse is always something you choose.

The app knows what each pack should contain and tells you when something is missing.

The game reads seven kinds of packs, each from its respective folder in the game files.

Voice and dub packs
packs_voice. A video and its dubbing clips. Can either be a normal voice pack or a dub voice pack.
Players
packs_player. Includes a picture, color pair, and up to nine reaction sounds.
Hosts
packs_host. The presenter's voice lines and portrait.
Judges
packs_judges. The judging panel's pictures and lines.
Studios
packs_studio. The 3D environment for the studio mode, as a .glb model.
Menus
packs_menu. Backgrounds, music and styling for the game's menus.
Chatter
packs_chatter. Sounds triggered by twitch chat.

Every pack you open is checked if it has all the required parts. A red mark means the game will not use it as intended and orange is something you should take note of.

Every line has its own controls in the list on the right: which take to use, how loud it is, and how far to nudge its timing.

  • Burn captions draws the script onto the video permanently. Leave it off and tick the .srt box instead if you would rather add captions in an editor.
  • Normalise dub loudness evens out lines recorded too quietly or too loudly.
  • Selected line only exports a single line as a short clip. Click a line first.

Exports can be queued.

  • No packs listed. Check Settings to see if the manager is finding the game folder.
  • A pack shows "no dubs yet". You have not recorded dubs yet. Do that in the game first.
  • A pack will not open. It is still converting.
  • Preview is black. Leave the preview and go back in. If it continues, use Report Bug/Suggestions in the app.
  • Export failed. Usually a full disk or a folder you cannot write to.
